READERCOMPANION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Overview

readerCompanion is a local-first desktop application for managing a personal e-book library on Windows and Linux. Its core features work without any account. The application has no server of its own: it sends no analytics or telemetry, and none of your data is sent to the developer.

Information the application processes

The application processes the book files and folders you choose to add, and the details it reads from them or you enter: titles, authors, series, genres, covers, reading status, dates, ratings and notes. This library is stored in a database on your computer.

If you use the optional integrations, it also processes the Goodreads export file you choose to import, and the Google Drive files and folders you choose to add.

Google Drive access

Connecting Google Drive is optional. In the application's settings you choose one of three access levels before you sign in, and Google asks for your consent to exactly that level:

  • Only books I pick (drive.file): the application can see only the files you pick in Google's file picker.
  • Whole Drive, read only (drive.readonly): the application can list your Drive's folders so you can choose some, and read the e-book files in the folders you choose.
  • Whole Drive, read and write (drive): as above, and, only when you ask for it, the application can rename and move e-book files in the folders you chose, create folders for them, move folders it emptied that way to your Google Drive trash, and move files you choose to delete to your Google Drive trash.

The application uses this access only to find your e-books, read their details and covers, open them on your computer, and carry out the file actions you ask for. It downloads a book's file to your computer to read its details or when you open it; downloaded copies stay on your computer.

readerCompanion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Sign-in information

Signing in happens in your web browser, on Google's own page; the application never sees your Google password. To stay connected it keeps a refresh token on your computer: in the system keyring (such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet) on Linux when one is available, encrypted for your Windows account on Windows, and otherwise in a file that only your user account can read.

Unlinking Google Drive in the application revokes this token at Google and deletes it from your computer. You can also revoke the application's access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Network connections

The application connects to other services only for features you use:

  • Google, to sign in and to work with your Google Drive files, when you link Google Drive.
  • Open Library, to look up missing book covers, only after you turn on online covers in the settings.

Importing from Goodreads reads a file you downloaded yourself; the application does not connect to Goodreads.

Storage, retention and deletion

Everything the application stores (the library database, covers, backups you make and downloaded copies of Drive books) is kept in its data folder on your computer, shown in the application's settings, for as long as you keep it. Deleting that folder removes it. Google Drive files stay in your Google Drive; the application changes them only when you ask it to.

Your choices

You can use readerCompanion without Google Drive or Goodreads, choose the narrowest Drive access, unlink Google Drive at any time, and back up or delete your local library.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
