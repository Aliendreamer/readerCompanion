readerCompanion

A simpler way to organize your personal e-book library.

About the application

readerCompanion is a local-first desktop application for managing EPUB and PDF books. Browse by author, series, or genre; find duplicates; organize files; and track what you've read or want to read.

You can use it offline without creating an account. Optional integrations include importing a Goodreads library export and linking selected Google Drive files or folders.

Google Drive integration

Connecting Google Drive is optional. You choose how much the application may see: only the books you pick in Google's file picker, or your whole Drive, read only or read and write. It uses this access to add the e-books from the files and folders you choose to your library and, only when you ask, to organise those files in Drive or move them to the Drive trash. You can unlink Drive at any time, which revokes the application's access. See the Privacy Policy for details.
